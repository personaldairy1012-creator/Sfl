No excuses

Mujhse galti hui.

Main maanta hoon, Roblox ko lekar mujhe insecurity hui. Maine 2 hours wali baat boli, aur jab aap usse zyada kheli toh mujhe laga ki meri baat ko importance nahi mili. Mujhe hurt hua.

Lekin hurt hone ke baad jo maine kiya — argument, gussa, aur sabse badi baat “break up” bol diya — woh sahi nahi tha.

“Gusse mein boli hui ek baat, humare poore relationship se badi nahi ho sakti.”

What I actually wanted

Game se zyada… aap.

Sach mein mujhe game se problem nahi hai. Mujhe bas us moment mein laga ki meri baat ignore ho rahi hai, aur main us feeling ko calmly explain nahi kar paaya.

But aapko control karna ya aapki happiness decide karna mera right nahi hai. Main aapse trust ke saath baat karna seekhna chahta hoon.

Trust 🤍Insecurity ke bajaye trust.
Communication 🫶Gusse ke bajaye baat.
Patience 🌷Instant reaction ke bajaye samajhna.
Us ❤️Problem ke against dono ek side.
And then you did this...

Aapka effort. 🥺

Aapne jo Canva edit banaya, usme jo feelings likhi… honestly, maine usse lightly nahi liya.

Usme “I still choose us”, less ego, less misunderstandings, more patience, more love — ye sab padh ke mujhe realize hua ki hum dono ko ek dusre se ladna nahi, problem se ladna hai.

Aapne itna effort kiya… ab meri baari hai. ❤️

One thing I need you to know

I don't want to lose you.

“Maine breakup gusse mein bol diya tha. Dil se nahi.”

Main aapse pyaar karta hoon. Bohot zyada. Aur main aapke bina future imagine nahi karna chahta.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki hum kabhi nahi ladenge. Ladenge. Kabhi main galat hounga, kabhi aap. But main chahta hoon ki har baar hum ek dusre ko punish karne ke bajaye samajhne ki koshish karein.

My promise

Less ego. More love.

Next time mujhe kuch bura lagega, main “break up” jaisi baat gusse mein nahi bolunga.

Main pehle calm hounga, phir aapse baat karunga. Aur aapki side bhi sununga — properly.

Aapko bhi meri feelings samajhne ki koshish karni hai, aur mujhe aapki freedom aur feelings ki respect karni hai.

Perfect nahi banna… bas ek dusre ke liye better banna hai. ❤️
